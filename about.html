Our story

A home for good design.

Ember & Oak Interiors is a small South African retail business with a big belief in local making, conscious materials, and pieces worth keeping.

“We want every piece to carry a trace of the hands and landscape that made it.”

Founded in Cape Town

Craftsmanship, with a contemporary eye.

Ember & Oak Interiors was founded in 2021 by interior designer Thabo Nkosi. Based in Woodstock, Cape Town, we source exclusively from local artisans across the Western Cape — bringing authentic African craftsmanship into considered, contemporary spaces.

01 / Our mission

Make conscious living feel beautiful.

To bring sustainable, artisan-crafted home furnishings to South African homes — supporting local craftspeople while promoting responsible, conscious living.

02 / Our vision

Be a destination for pieces with soul.

To be South Africa's premier online and in-store destination for unique, handcrafted, eco-conscious home interiors.

Who we serve

  • Homeowners aged 25–55 seeking distinctive décor.
  • Interior designers and property stylists sourcing unique pieces.
  • Environmentally conscious consumers who prioritise sustainable products.
  • Corporate clients furnishing boutique offices and hospitality spaces.
